Frequently asked questions


    
What is an .ics file?

    
An .ics file is the standard calendar format (RFC 5545) used to
    move events between calendars. Import it into Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple
    Calendar, or Thunderbird and the event appears in your calendar.


    
Is ics-gen free?

    
Yes — free and open source (MIT), no sign-up and no account required.


    
Does ics-gen upload my event data?

    
No. The page runs 100% in your browser with no server and no third-party
    requests — nothing you type ever leaves your device.


    
Does ics-gen work offline?

    
Yes. Open index.html directly from disk, or host the folder on
    any static site — no build step and no network calls.


    
Can I create recurring events or reminders?

    
Yes — daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly recurrence with optional end dates,
    configurable reminders (VALARM), plus all-day, timed, and multi-day events
    with attendees.
